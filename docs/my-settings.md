# My settings

**My settings** holds the settings that are yours: your account, the messages the academy sends you, how the app looks for you, and what you share. Open it from the menu under your name.

It opens on an overview with up to four groups. Each group has one line that tells you how it stands right now, so you can see at a glance whether something needs changing. Tap a group to open it. The breadcrumb at the top, **My settings**, takes you back to the overview.

On a computer (a screen at least 1024 pixels wide), the list of groups stays on the left and the open group sits on the right.

A group only shows when it has something for you. A player sees the parent-visibility choices only when a parent is linked to their record, and only staff who run matches see the live match screen setting.

## Account

Your profile (first and last name, display name, email and phone number), your password, and two-factor authentication.

- The summary line shows your display name, your email address and whether two-factor authentication is on.
- The profile and the password each have their own **Save** and **Cancel**. Cancel takes you back to the overview without saving.
- A player's display name is set by the academy from their name and can't be changed here.
- Changing your password signs you out on your other devices.
- **Two-factor authentication** shows whether it is on. The button opens the screen where you set it up or manage it.

## Messages

Which messages the academy may send you. Unticking one stops it on every channel. Only the messages that can reach you are listed.

- The summary line counts how many are switched on, for example "9 of 12 on".
- Some messages are always sent and can't be switched off: safeguarding messages, messages about getting back into your account, and cancelled trainings.
- Alerts about your own data are set separately, under alert settings; the Messages page links to them.

## Appearance

How the app looks for you: the navigation layout, the visual theme and, for staff who run matches, the live match screen layout. Each one can follow the academy default or be set for you alone.

- The summary line shows the layout and the theme you see now, for example "App · theme Default".
- A theme changes colours, corners and heading type, never what you can see or do.

## Privacy

What you share and what the academy holds about you.

- **What your parent can see** (players with a linked parent): choose which parts of your record your parent or guardian can see. Everything is shared until you switch a part off. Coaches and the academy are not affected. The summary line says "Your parent sees everything" or how many parts are hidden. A signed development talk follows the development plan switch, the trainer's scores on it included; the same scores, kept as an evaluation, follow the evaluations switch.
- **What the academy holds about you**: every contact detail the academy holds about you, and where each one is kept. It is read-only. Your account details are changed under Account; anything on your child's file is changed by the academy.

## Saving

Every form saves on its own **Save** button, and you stay on the same group page afterwards with a message that the change was saved.
