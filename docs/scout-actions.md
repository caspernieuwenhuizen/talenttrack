# Scout — the season in actions

A scout's work in TalentTrack is the top of the funnel: find a player, log what
you saw, and hand the academy enough context that the head of development can
act on it without ringing you.

If you are new here, read [Getting started](getting-started.md) first.

## Logging what you find

### Log a new prospect

Use **+ New prospect** from your dashboard or from the
[onboarding pipeline](onboarding-pipeline.md). The wizard asks for who the
player is, where you saw them, and how to reach a parent.

Two fields carry more weight than the rest:

- **Discovery context** — where and in what circumstances you saw them. This
  travels with the player for as long as they are at the academy. In two years,
  when someone asks why this player was signed, this is the answer.
- **What you saw** — your actual read. Specific beats generous: "comfortable
  receiving on the half-turn under pressure" is worth more than "good
  technique".

Logging a prospect automatically raises a task on the head of development to
invite them to a test training, so you do not need to chase anyone.

### Consent

Record parent consent when you have it. These are minors, and the consent state
travels with the record — see the
[privacy operator guide](privacy-operator-guide.md) for what the academy is
committing to.

## Following your prospects

### Watch the pipeline

The [onboarding pipeline](onboarding-pipeline.md) is the kanban view of the
funnel: who is where, and what is waiting. It is scoped to you, so what you see
is your own intake rather than the whole academy's.

### Review your portfolio

The **Prospects overview** (`?tt_view=prospects-overview`) lists your prospects
with their current stage. Use it before a conversation with the head of
development, or as a weekly catch-up.

Look for two things: prospects that have gone quiet at one stage, and which
kinds of discovery context actually convert. The second one changes where you
spend your Saturdays.

### Follow a test-training outcome

When the head of development records the outcome of a test training, the result
shows on the prospect's card in the pipeline — whether they came, and whether
they are moving to the trial group or being declined.

Prospects admitted to a trial group become player records, and their trial runs
through [trials](trials.md) from there.

## Planning the weekend

### Plan scouting visits

Scouting visits (`?tt_view=scouting-visits`) let you plan which matches you are
going to and record what came of each trip. Planning them in the system means
the academy can see coverage — which age groups and which regions are actually
being watched — instead of it living in your head.

## What you cannot do yet

Two things scouts reasonably expect are not built:

- **Adding a follow-up note to a prospect you already logged.** Notes are
  captured when you create the prospect and cannot be appended to afterwards.
- **Correcting parent contact details or consent on an existing prospect.**
  The wizard is create-only.

Until those exist, put corrections and later observations in the message to the
head of development rather than assuming the record will carry them. Both are
on the backlog.
