# Finding something in your child's record

A coach texts you that a session is "in TalentTrack now". This is how you find it.

## The search field

The magnifier in the header, beside the help icon. Type a few letters and press Enter, or open it with nothing typed and search from the page itself.

It searches four kinds of record, for your own children and nobody else's:

- **Activities** — trainings, matches and other sessions your child is on, including ones still to come.
- **Evaluations** — what a coach wrote about your child.
- **Goals** — what your child is working on.
- **Messages** — what the academy sent you.

## What you can type

- **A name**, or part of one. "Training", "Ajax", "Trainingsveld".
- **A date**. "3 Nov", "2026-11-03" and "3 november" all work. A date matches activities on that day, evaluations recorded on it, goals due on it, and messages sent on it.

Two characters is the shortest search that returns anything. Results open the record straight away, and the back link brings you back to your results.

## What it will not find

Only your own children's records. The search never reaches another family, and it never tells you that another family's record exists: a search that would match somebody else's child returns exactly what a search for a made-up name returns — nothing at all, with no count and no suggestion.

It also never shows you something you could not already open from your child's own pages. It makes what you are entitled to reachable; it does not widen it.

## If you have more than one child

The page says whose records it is searching, and it searches all of them at once. Each result opens on the right child.

## Seeing everything that is coming up

Your child's activities page opens on what has already happened, because that is what most people are looking for. **Show upcoming** at the top of the list flips it to everything still to come, soonest first, and the date filter works there too. The **Coming up** block above the list always shows the next few, whatever mode the list is in.
