# Player report: printed copy and PDF (#3874)

The player report now prints. Choose a **One-pager** — one A4 to bring to a
conversation — or a **Two-page pack** with room for every evaluation, match and
test, and **Download PDF**. When the one-pager needs room, long lists keep their
most recent entries and say how many more there are, the development plan
becomes a single line, and the notes area keeps three lines; the conversation
set fits one page for a typical player. **Printed size** shows the page count
the PDF will have, measured against DomPDF's real output so the panel and the
paper agree. The PDF shows each reader only what the report shows them on
screen.
