# A teammate's card is no longer a one-way door (#3395)

Tapping a teammate on **My team** opened a card with no breadcrumb trail and no
back link — the browser's back button was the only way out. Every other player
screen has carried the trail for releases; this one was listed in the standards
as a sub-view while being reachable by its own address.

The teammate card now shows the trail back to the dashboard, and arriving from
My team — from the roster or from the podium — offers a **← Back to My team**
link as well.
