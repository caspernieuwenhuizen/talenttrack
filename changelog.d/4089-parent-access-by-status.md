# Parents of a trialist see the schedule and attendance; a graduated player's parents read, and are sent nothing (#4089)

What a parent reaches now depends on their child's status, decided in one place and asked by every parent screen, REST route and message. A child on trial appears on the parent's dashboard with only their trainings, matches and attendance: evaluations, goals, the development plan and every other staff judgement stay closed until the child signs, every other child screen says so, and every other per-player route answers 403. Messages about a trialist's development (the goal nudge, the plan-ready message, the inactive-parent nudge) no longer go to the family; the trial welcome and schedule messages still do. A graduated player's parents keep reading what they could read before, cannot reply in conversations or acknowledge a development-plan talk (with a message that says why), and are sent nothing more about the child, the player's own account included. Active and released children are unchanged. `GET /me` now lists trial and graduated children too, each with an `access` of `full`, `trial` or `read_only`. The parent search no longer finds evaluations or goals of a child who has hidden them from their parents, and the parent dashboard's "new evaluations" count respects that choice too.
