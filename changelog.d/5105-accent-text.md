# Gold text on the default theme is readable: podium, view chips, percentages, 404 (#5105)

The default theme printed its gold as text on white at about 1.9:1 in four
places: a team link on the podium when you hover or focus it, the star on a
saved view, the percentage next to a popularity bar, and the 404 emblem.
Those now use a darker shade of the same gold that reads at 5.4:1. Gold
fills, borders and markers are unchanged. An academy that sets its own
secondary colour under Branding gets the same treatment: the colour still
drives every fill, and where it is printed as text it is darkened just
enough to read.
