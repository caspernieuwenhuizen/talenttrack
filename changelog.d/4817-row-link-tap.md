# Dashboard table widgets: row links are big enough to tap (#4817)

The links in the dashboard's table widgets, such as a player's name under
Behaviour pending or "Open" under My recent prospects, were 21px tall on a
phone, too small to hit reliably with a finger. On a touch screen they are now
at least 48 by 48 pixels; with a mouse they are at least 36 pixels tall, the
same sizes the shared buttons use.
