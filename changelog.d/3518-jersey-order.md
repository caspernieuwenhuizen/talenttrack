# Monthly report: player tables read in squad-number order (#3518)

The team monthly report now lists players by squad number — on screen and on the
printed copy — so a player sits in the same place in every table instead of
moving between the roster, the attendance list and the test results. Players
without a squad number come last, alphabetically.

The two tables where the order carries the finding are unchanged: minutes share
still reads by share played, and the players needing a conversation still read
most urgent first.
