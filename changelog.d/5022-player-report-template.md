# Report Studio: the player report as a template, for staff, a scout and the family (#5022)

Bump: minor

The player report is now a Report Studio template, once for each reader: staff, a scout and the family. Each opens on the sections the player report opens on for that reader, in its order, and every block shows and prints exactly what that section of the player report shows. Every section of the player report is now a Studio block, including evaluations, attendance, playing time, goals, the notes area and the staff notes; who may receive each follows the player report's allowlists. A note written on the notes area is stored on the report's snapshot, never in the report. `GET /report-templates` lists the templates a builder may start: the scout version needs the right to publish reports.
