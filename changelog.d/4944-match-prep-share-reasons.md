# Match prep share link shows absence reasons again (#4944)

The match prep share link once again lists each unavailable player as
"name · status (reason)". The link is meant for staff only, and the coach
decides who gets it. A logged-in reader who may not see the reasons still gets
the status alone, on the screen, the print and the PDF. The match preparation
help, in English and Dutch, now says the link carries the reasons and should go
to staff only.
