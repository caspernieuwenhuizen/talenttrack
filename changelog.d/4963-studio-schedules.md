# Report Studio: send a published report on a schedule (#4963)

Bump: minor

A published Report Studio report can now be sent by email as a PDF, every week, every month or at the end of the season. On the report, choose **Schedule**. Recipients are accounts and staff roles only. A coach may schedule a report for themselves; sending it to others needs the right to publish reports. Every recipient gets the same PDF, so it holds only what every one of them may read. **What they get** shows before you save which blocks they get and which are left out, and why. A schedule always sends the published version, is changed in place with Save and Cancel, and pauses, telling the person who set it up once, when the report is archived or unpublished. Every change to a schedule is kept in the audit log.
