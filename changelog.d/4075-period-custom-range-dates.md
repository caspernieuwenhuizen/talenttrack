# Period filter: usable custom-range dates, and "Custom range" listed once (#4075)

Opening Custom range… in the Period menu no longer squeezes the From and To dates to a few pixels: the menu widens, the two dates sit side by side at a usable size, and Apply gets its own row underneath. The menu also stops offering a second "Custom range" preset above the real one, which went back to the default window rather than opening a custom range, so no row is highlighted when no preset is chosen. Standard reports keep their default-window preset.
