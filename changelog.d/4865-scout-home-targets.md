# Scout dashboard on a phone: "Show all" and "Plan visit" are full tap targets (#4865)

On the scout's dashboard, the scouting plan's "Show all" link was 24px tall and its "Plan visit" button 40px. On a phone both are now at least 48px tall, the same as the other dashboard widgets' "Show all" links. With a mouse, "Plan visit" keeps its 40px height.
