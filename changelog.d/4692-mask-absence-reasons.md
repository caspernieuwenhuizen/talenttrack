# Absence reasons follow the sensitive-field registry (#4692)

Why a child missed or will miss a training or a match now goes only to staff of the player, the player themselves and a linked parent. That covers the register's note, the note beside a planned status, and the coach's reason beside a match-prep availability status. Any other reader of the activity, such as the read-only observer, keeps the status (present, absent, excused and so on) and gets no reason. The planned-attendance route leaves the note out, the match preparation's screen, printout and PDF leave the reason empty, the activity brief PDF prints "Hidden" where a note would be, and the attendance register file leaves the cell empty. A shared match-prep link lists each status without the reason. Staff and family see what they saw before.
