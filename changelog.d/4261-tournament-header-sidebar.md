# Tournament detail: the minutes sidebar no longer covers the header (#4261)

On a desktop-width screen the fixed Minutes sidebar on a tournament's detail
page covered the top-right of the page, cutting off "Add match" and hiding
"Edit". The header now sits inside the area that already makes room for the
sidebar, so the title and both actions stay fully visible and clickable. The
phone layout, with the minutes strip at the bottom, is unchanged.
