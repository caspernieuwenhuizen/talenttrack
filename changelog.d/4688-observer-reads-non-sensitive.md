# Read-only observer reads the non-sensitive entities; PDP stays closed on every route (#4688)

Bump: minor

The read-only observer now reads everything in the academy that is not sensitive, academy-wide and read-only: measurements, tournaments and each player's tournament record, training plans and load, the exercise library, VCT, media, and the academy's reference data (seasons, methodology, football actions, evaluation categories and weights, the rating scale, holidays, the help pages). Records that still carry a field the sensitive-field registry cannot mask (attendance, the evaluations list, status and potential, trials, prospects and scouting, custom player fields, the people list, the roster and player lists) follow together with their masking. Injuries, safeguarding notes, player and staff-only notes, message threads, parent accounts, the PDP cycle, the audit log and Report Studio stay closed. The observer holds no create, change or delete right anywhere. `GET /players/{id}/pdp` asks read on the player's PDP section, and the role carries no raw `tt_view_pdp` capability, so PDP data is closed to the observer on every route. Existing installs get the new rows through migration 0310, which leaves every observer cell an academy already changed as it is.
