# Minutes share and the monthly report show one median (#4993)

The minutes share report and the team monthly report now take the squad's median minutes share in the same way: from the unrounded shares, rounded once. For a squad with an even number of players the two could differ by 0.1 before. The figures that can move by 0.1 are in the team monthly report: the median minutes share in its minutes section and its "Median minutes share" headline tile, both on screen and in the PDF. The Report Studio blocks that show this median move with them. An empty Minutes share report no longer says a team has no played matches at all when the team did play, outside the period, but has no minutes recorded for those matches; it says so and points to the Activities log. Counting the matches outside the period no longer reads every one of them.
