# Dashboard: the Recent comments widget shows again, and only what the reader may open (#4842)

The Recent comments & notes widget asked for a permission no role holds, so it was hidden from everyone, coaches included. It now follows the conversation rules themselves: each comment is shown only if the reader may open its thread, and a staff-only note only to those who may read staff-only notes. A head coach sees the latest comments on their own teams' threads; a parent or a read-only observer sees only the comments their access allows.
