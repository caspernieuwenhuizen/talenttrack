# Player report: a scout version, and one report engine for staff and scouts (#3876)

The player report now knows who it is written for. Staff read the full report;
a scout reads a shorter version on the players assigned to them: the header,
evaluation scores without the coach's written notes, attendance, playing time
and tests at the public level. That is decided from who is reading, not from
what is asked for, and whatever a scout is not meant to see is left out of the
data itself, not just hidden on screen.

The scout's **My players** view, the scouting report emailed from **Generate
report**, its preview and the scouting report PDF now all show this same scout
version. **Generate report** no longer offers the staff audiences. It links to
the player report instead and keeps the parent, player and scout reports. The
scouting report PDF also now checks that the person downloading it may read a
report on that player.
