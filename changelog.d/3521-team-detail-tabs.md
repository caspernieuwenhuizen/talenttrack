# The team page splits into Overview and Statistics (#3521)

The team page stacked eight panels on one scroll — key facts, at a glance,
roster, staff, team info, trial roster, upcoming activities, media — and had
nowhere left to put a ninth.

It now carries two tabs under the team's name. **Overview** is the page exactly
as it was, in the same order, still governed by each coach's own Customize
choice. **Statistics** is where a team's record, form and leaderboards land in
the next release; until then it says so rather than opening onto a blank panel.

Two tabs, not one per panel: the section list stays under Customize, where it
has always been, because eight tabs for eight cards would read worse than the
scroll they replaced.

Switching tabs reloads the page, which is the point — opening a roster no longer
has to pay for statistics nobody asked for. An old bookmark with no tab, or an
unrecognised one, opens Overview, and the "back to…" link you arrived with
survives the switch.

The pinned team name moved up the page to carry the tabs. It now stays with you
from the top of the page rather than arriving halfway down it.
