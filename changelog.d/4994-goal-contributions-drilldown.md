# Goals and assists: every match behind a count, and where each entry came from (#4994)

Bump: minor

A coach who sees a goals or assists number can now check where it comes from and find a wrong entry. On the team's Statistics tab each player's goals and assists open a drill-down. A new standard report, Team · Goals and assists (per team and season, on the Reports page), carries the same table and the same drill-down. For every match behind the count, the drill-down shows the date, opponent, home or away, score and a link to the match. It lists each goal with its minute (none for a goal entered after the match), scorer and assister, plus where the entry came from, when, and who entered it. A match is marked when it holds more assists or attributed goals than we scored, when a player is over the ceiling the minutes grid refuses writes at, or when an assist sits on an opponent goal or an own goal. Staff who may edit get a link to the minutes grid to correct it. The drill-down's matches add up to the tapped number: the Statistics tab, the report, the new `GET /teams/{id}/players/{player_id}/goal-contributions` route and the Studio block all count with one rule. A migration adds `source`, `created_by`, `updated_at` and `updated_by` to the goal log, and every writer now stamps them. Older rows read as "recorded before tracking began", with no invented author.
