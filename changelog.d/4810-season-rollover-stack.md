# Season rollover on a phone: every team's line fits on the screen (#4810)

On a phone the season rollover lists each team as a card. A long team name and
the "Promote to" choice pushed the card wider than the screen, so the team
name, its age group and its player count ran off the edge out of sight. The
cards now fit at every phone width, with each value next to its label and the
choice shrinking to the space that is left. The tables also move onto the
shared stacked-table layout, so a screen reader hears them as tables with
their column headers. From 768px up nothing changes.
