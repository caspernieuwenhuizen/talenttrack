# People lists show "Hidden" for a guardian's contact (#4715)

A guardian's e-mail and phone on the people list, a person's page, the wp-admin people list and a team's staff list now follow the same rule as the people REST routes: the guardian themselves and staff of one of their children see them; any other reader of people, such as the read-only observer, sees "Hidden" in their place. The list's rows carry the e-mail cell as `email_cell_html`, either the link or the "Hidden" marker, so the screen can say what is hidden without receiving it. The people search and the e-mail sort use the address only for a reader who may read every guardian's contact. The person form is for a reader who may edit people, anyone else gets the person's page, and its account list holds this club's accounts. The mail composer does not open for a recipient whose address is hidden from the sender. Staff see what they saw before.
