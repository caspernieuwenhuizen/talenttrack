# Dashboard on a phone: every folded widget says what it holds (#4827)

The table widgets already folded under their own title on a phone. Now the
other dashboard widgets do too: My tasks, Recent evaluations, Team overview, a
team's roster, Up next, a KPI and the rest show the same title on a phone fold
as in their heading on a larger screen, instead of a generic widget name. The
headings themselves are unchanged.
