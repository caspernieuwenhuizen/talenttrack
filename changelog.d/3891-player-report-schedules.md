# Player report: scheduled monthly, for a team or a player (#3891)

A head of development can now have player reports arrive on the 1st of every
month: **Schedule monthly** on the player report sends either the whole team —
one report per player, each from its own page, in one PDF, in shirt-number order
— or a single player. The schedule keeps its own copy of the sections and the
period, and renders as the person who set it up. It stops rather than sends when
the team or player is archived or its owner can no longer read them; in a team
round, a single player the owner can no longer read is left out and named on the
schedules screen, never dropped silently. A round covers at most 30 players.
