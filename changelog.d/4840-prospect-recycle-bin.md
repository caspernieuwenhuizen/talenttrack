# Prospects can be deleted: recycle bin, restore and purge (#4840)

A prospect can be deleted from their page and from the focus panel on the onboarding board, after a confirm, by anyone who may delete prospects (scouts, the head of development and the academy admin by default). The prospect goes to the recycle bin with their observations at scouting visits, consent requests and workflow tasks. Restoring puts the prospect back where they were: on the board, or archived when they were archived already. A purge, by hand or after the bin's retention period, removes the prospect with those records, and the bin's preview counts them first; the prospect retention purge removes the same list. A prospect who has become a player or has a trial case is refused with a 409 and is deleted through the player. `DELETE /prospects/{id}` is the REST route. Migration 0330 adds the recycle-bin columns to `tt_prospects`.
