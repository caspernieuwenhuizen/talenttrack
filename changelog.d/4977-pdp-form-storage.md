# PDP conversation forms store both scores, and sign-off writes the evaluation and the goals (#4977)

Bump: minor

A PDP conversation held on a conversation form now keeps the player's and the trainer's scores and answers, each part saving itself as it is filled in. The player's part stays the player's own until they mark their preparation ready, and closes when the talk starts. Signing the form off saves the trainer's scores as an evaluation linked to the conversation and turns the two development points into goals, all at once or not at all; an evaluation saved this way cannot be edited on its own. This is the groundwork the player's and the trainer's form screens build on; there is no new screen yet.
