# Player journey: families see their own trial start and end (#4828)

The player and their linked parents see "Trial started" and "Trial ended" in the player's own journey, with the date only, while the child shares the journey. The trial case, its notes, decision and letters stay behind the trial read right, which families do not hold. Entries about records a reader may not open stay out of the journey and out of its "hidden" count.
