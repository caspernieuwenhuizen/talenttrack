# Players and PDP lists on a phone: the row is the one target (#4863)

On a phone, the team name in a row of the players list and the PDP list is now plain text. Tapping anywhere on the row opens the player or the PDP. Before, the team name was its own 18px-tall link inside the row and caught taps meant for the row. On a tablet or desktop the team name is still a link.
