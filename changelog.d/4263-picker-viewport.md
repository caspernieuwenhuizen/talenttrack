# Match prep's player picker opens on screen when the page is scrolled (#4263)

Picking a player for a line-up position or a set-piece role in match prep
opened the "Search player…" box below the visible area once the page was
scrolled, while its dark backdrop blocked everything else, so on a phone a
coach could not place players at all. The box now opens next to what you
tapped, always inside the screen, and at 360 px it is never wider than the
screen.
