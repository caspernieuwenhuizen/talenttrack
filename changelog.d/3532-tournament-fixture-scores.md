# Tournament fixtures can record their results (#3532)

Bump: minor

A tournament day with five fixtures had nowhere to record any of their results.
The table behind it carried the opponent, the formation, the duration and the
substitution windows — and no score at all, so a tournament read in the product
as attendance and minutes with no outcome attached.

Each fixture in the match programme now carries **Ours** and **Theirs**, saved
as you move off the box.

This is deliberately *not* the single scoreline a league match has. A tournament
day is several matches and one score cannot describe it, which is why a
tournament still gets no Result card and no score boxes in the minutes grid. The
result belongs to the fixture, so that is where it lives.

Leaving a box empty records **no result**, not 0–0 — a fixture you have not
played, or one nobody typed a score into, reads as played-without-a-result.

There is no day total. The team's record leaves tournaments out entirely, so a
goals-for/against across the day would have nothing reading it yet.

Goals scored at a tournament already counted for the player who scored them, and
still do; that half never depended on these boxes.
