# Buttons: 48 px under touch, never under 36 px with a mouse (#4310)

The shared button now carries both of its height floors in one place, next to the canonical button styles: 48 px under a coarse pointer (phones, tablets), and a 36 px floor under a fine pointer, where it used to come out at about 39 px with nothing holding it. Both read new `--tt-tap-min` / `--tt-tap-min-fine` design tokens. About seventy per-screen rules that pinned individual buttons to 48 px on every pointer are gone in favour of the shared rule, so desktop screens keep a consistent, denser button height while touch screens keep the 48 px target. Two buttons that sit in one row with a 48 px field keep their own height, with the reason beside the rule. The player profile's own button rules are unchanged.
