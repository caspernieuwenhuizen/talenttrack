# Player profile: the Activities tab shows only completed activities (#4968)

The Activities tab on a player's profile now lists what happened: completed
activities dated today or earlier, newest first, for staff, parents and the
player alike. Upcoming trainings and matches no longer sit at the top with a
"Planned" pill, and a past activity whose plan was never recorded stays off it
too; both remain on the Activities list and the calendar. The tab's badge
counts the same activities, so the number and the list still agree. The
attendance figure on the profile is unchanged.
