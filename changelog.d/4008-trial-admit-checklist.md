# Trial cases: a follow-up checklist after an admit (#4008)

Recording ADMIT on a trial case now adds a Follow-up card to the case's Overview tab with three steps that used to live in people's heads: the admittance letter sent, the player added to a team, and a guardian's contact collected. Each step ticks itself when the data says it has happened (a live admittance letter, the player on an academy team, a guardian e-mail on record), can be ticked by hand, shows who ticked it and when, and can name the staff member who does it. The whole panel sees it; only case managers change it, and every change is audit-logged. Other decisions get no checklist, and nothing is sent or scheduled. Available over REST as `GET /trial-cases/{id}/checklist` and `PATCH /trial-cases/{id}/checklist/{item_key}`. Migration 0293 adds the table.
