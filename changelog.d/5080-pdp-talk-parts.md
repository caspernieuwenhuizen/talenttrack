# PDP talk: two parts with their own save state, and no duplicate fields on a form talk (#5080)

A PDP talk's Conversation tab now shows two visibly separate parts, each a card with its number, title and its own save state and undo in the header: "1 Conversation details" (the dates and the goals discussed) and "2 Conversation form · Outfield player / Goalkeeper". Each undo reaches only its own part. The form's header stays in view while the trainer scrolls through the sections, just below the stuck record spine, whose height the spine now publishes as `--tt-spine-h`; on a phone the save state wraps under the title, and below 480px the spine leaves out its talk line (the hero carries it) and moves the form name and switch out of the stuck strip into the page just under it, so the stuck chrome stays within 45% of a 360x640 screen. On a talk with a conversation form, part 1 no longer asks for "Notes (post-meeting)" or "Agreed actions", since the form has a note per section and the development points; an older form talk that already has text in them shows it read only. Talks without a form keep both fields. Sign-off and the other irreversible steps sit under their own "Finish" heading, below both parts.
