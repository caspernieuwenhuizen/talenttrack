# PDP talk: two parts with their own save state, and no duplicate fields on a form talk (#5080)

A PDP talk's Conversation tab now shows two visibly separate parts, each a card with its number, title and its own save state and undo in the header: "1 Conversation details" (the dates and the goals discussed) and "2 Conversation form · Outfield player / Goalkeeper". Each undo reaches only its own part. The form's header stays in view while the trainer scrolls through the sections, and on a phone the save state wraps under the title. On a talk with a conversation form, part 1 no longer asks for "Notes (post-meeting)" or "Agreed actions", since the form has a note per section and the development points; an older form talk that already has text in them shows it read only. Talks without a form keep both fields. Sign-off and the other irreversible steps sit under their own "Finish" heading, below both parts.
