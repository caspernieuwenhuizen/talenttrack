# Coach dashboard: the evaluation cards open the evaluations list, not a refusal (#5048)

A coach who tapped "My evaluations this week" on the dashboard landed on "you do not have access": the card opened the player's "My evaluations" view, which coaches cannot open. It now opens the evaluations list filtered to the evaluations the coach wrote over the same seven days, and the "players I evaluated this season" card does the same for the current season. The list's Coach filter now offers a coach their own name; filtering on another coach still needs the evaluation-coverage access the head of development has, and `GET /evaluations` refuses another coach's id with 403 for everyone else. The attendance and team-rating cards keep their links to the team attendance report and the team's evaluations. "My evaluations" stays the player and parent view, and its unused coach version is gone.

The "this week" card and its sparkline now count evaluations by their date, as the list it opens does, instead of by the day they were entered. An evaluation entered today for a match two weeks ago no longer counts on the card while missing from the list.
