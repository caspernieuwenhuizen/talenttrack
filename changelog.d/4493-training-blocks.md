# This week panel: training theme and attendance after the fact (#4493)

The This week panel has two new blocks under Trainings. **Training theme** shows the theme of the training plan linked to a training, as a grey chip, and nothing when no plan is linked. **Attendance after the fact** shows, on a past training whose attendance is recorded, how many of the recorded players were there, for example "12 of 14 present". Late counts as present, the same rule as everywhere else. By default the head coach sees both on a desktop and the assistant coach sees the theme. Neither shows on a phone unless the academy switches it on. Both are read for the whole week at once, so the panel adds no query per training. `GET me/week` returns them as `theme` and `attendance_rate`.
