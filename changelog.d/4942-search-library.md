# Search finds the library: exercises, training plans, methodology and courses (#4942)

Bump: minor

Staff now find the library from the search box, in a new Library group between Settings & modules and Sections. Exercises are found by name, description and category, the current version only and never an archived one. Training plans are found by title, notes and theme: a coach gets their own teams' plans and the club templates, the head of development every plan. Methodology covers the principles, sub-principles and learning goals of the methodology the academy selected. Courses are the ones the courses page lists, and a locked course is listed and marked Locked. Each kind follows the access rules of its own list and detail page, counts only what the viewer may open, and returns nothing while its module is switched off. Parents and players get no Library group.
