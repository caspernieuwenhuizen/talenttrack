# Player pills and the match prep roster: status text readable on every theme (#5130)

The trial, late and medium-priority pills on the player screens printed
their gold at about 3:1 on their pale yellow, the on-pitch minutes in the
match preparation printed their green at about 3:1, and the substitute
marks in the match preparation printed their orange at 4.2:1. They now use
the same darker status shades as the rest of the product, at 4.5:1 or better
on every theme. The pills' and cells' own backgrounds, and the gold border
on a trial player's photo, are unchanged. In team chemistry, the pale green
and red behind the strong and weak fit marks now follow the theme instead of
always showing the default theme's.
