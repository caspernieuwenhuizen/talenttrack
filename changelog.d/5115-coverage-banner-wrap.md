# Monthly report: the coverage banner shows the whole sentence (#5115)

The yellow banner at the top of the monthly team report, which says how many
completed activities have a register and which were never closed, was cut at
150 characters. In the PDF it was also one line, clipped at the right margin,
so a Dutch banner naming its activities lost its end. Every surface that shows
the report (the screen, print, the PDF and the Report Studio) now shows the
whole sentence, wrapped inside the page, and the PDF's page estimate counts the
lines it wraps to. The page count of a typical report is unchanged.
