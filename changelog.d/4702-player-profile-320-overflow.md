# Player profile no longer scrolls sideways on a 320px phone (#4702)

At 320px the player profile scrolled sideways by 6px. One long label, such as "Geboortedatum" in the key facts or "Aanwezigheid" in the at-a-glance tiles, could not shrink and widened its column past the screen. The three facts cells and the KPI tiles now share the row in equal columns, and a long label breaks inside its tile, hyphenated where the language allows. This is the same fix the team page's tiles already had. On a 360px or 390px phone, the columns are now equal width and some long labels hyphenate onto two lines. From 480px nothing changes. The team page shares the key-facts strip, so its 19px sideways scroll at 320px is gone as well. CSS only.
