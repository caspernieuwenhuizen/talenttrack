# Player profile no longer scrolls sideways on a 320px phone (#4702)

At 320px the player profile scrolled sideways by 6px. One long label, such as "Geboortedatum" in the key facts or "Aanwezigheid" in the at-a-glance tiles, could not shrink and widened its column past the screen. The three facts cells and the KPI tiles now share the row in equal columns, and a long label breaks inside its tile, hyphenated where the language allows. This is the same fix the team page's tiles already had. On a 360px phone this means the columns are equal and two or three labels now hyphenate. At 390px the columns are equal width, and the tiles are the same height as before. From 480px nothing changes. The team page shares the key-facts strip, so its 19px sideways scroll at 320px is gone as well. CSS only.
