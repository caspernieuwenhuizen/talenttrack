# Player profile no longer scrolls sideways on a 320px phone (#4702)

At 320px the player profile scrolled sideways by 6px. One long label, such as "Geboortedatum" in the key facts or "Aanwezigheid" in the at-a-glance tiles, could not shrink and widened its column past the screen. The three facts cells and the KPI tiles now share the row in equal columns, the same fix the team page's tiles already had. To fit those columns on a 360px or 390px phone without breaking a word, the labels use short forms. In Dutch these are Geboren, Lid sinds, Gem. cijfer, Aanwezig and Gescoord; in English, Attended and Tests. The full words used elsewhere in the plugin are unchanged. The team page shares the key-facts strip, so its 19px sideways scroll at 320px is gone as well.
