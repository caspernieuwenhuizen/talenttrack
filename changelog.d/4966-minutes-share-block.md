# Minutes share counts only matches with minutes recorded, on every report (#4966)

A player's share of the playing time now counts only the matches whose minutes were recorded, on the minutes-share report, its REST route and the Report Studio's **Share of available minutes** block, as the team monthly report, its key figures and the playing-time block already did. A played match nobody filled in minutes for used to count on the first three and not on the others, so they showed a lower share for every player: for one squad the block's median was 40.7% where the monthly report showed 81.4%. The Studio block now reads the monthly report's minutes section directly. On the minutes-share report, the **Matches played** tile still counts every played match, and a window whose played matches have no minutes recorded yet says so.
