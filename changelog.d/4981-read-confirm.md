# The player and the parent read and confirm a signed PDP conversation form (#4981)

Once the trainer signs off a PDP conversation form, the player and each parent who may see the development plan get one message. They read the whole talk in My PDP: a fold per section with both averages, both scores on every item, the trainer's notes, the agreed strongest quality and the two development points. They confirm it with "I have read it". A player who did not prepare reads "not filled in" in their own column.
