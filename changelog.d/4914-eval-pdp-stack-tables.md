# Evaluation and PDP tables on a phone: one card per row, every value labelled (#4914)

On a phone, the ratings on an evaluation, the coach's list of recent
evaluations, the PDP coverage by team and the PDP planning matrix now use the
shared stacked-table layout: one card per row, with each value next to its
column name. The PDP coverage by team was unreadable on a phone: its figures
sat off the right of the screen. Each team is now a card with the team as its
title. A screen reader hears each of these lists as a table with its column
headers. From 480 pixels wide they are tables, as before.
