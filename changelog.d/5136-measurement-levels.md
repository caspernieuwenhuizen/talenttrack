# Measurement level colours follow the selected theme (#5136)

The green, amber, red, blue and grey measurement levels used the default
theme's colours under every theme. They now take the chosen theme's status
colours, like the rest of the product. On the default theme the level colours
themselves stay the same, and the other level colours (lime, yellow, orange, cyan) have fixed values
and stay as they are. The value chips on a player's Measurements tab now read
on every theme: the green, amber, red and blue chips are painted in a darker
shade of their colour so their white text reads at 4.5:1 or better (a visible
change, also on the default theme, where the amber chip becomes a deep amber
brown with white text), and the orange chip's text is now dark instead of
white. In team chemistry, the hint on the sandbox banner ("Tap
any slot on the pitch…") was pale grey on the banner's teal tint and hard to
read; it is now in the normal text colour.
