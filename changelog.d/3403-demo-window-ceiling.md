# Demo history window: three years, and a clamp that says so (#3403)

The per-run **weeks of history** ceiling goes from 104 to 156 — three years
rather than two, which is a full academy arc and what the new multi-season
shape and four-rounds-a-season cadence were built for. The teams and
players-per-team ceilings are unchanged; nothing argued for more of either.

Asking for more than a ceiling allows no longer reduces the number in silence.
The run goes ahead with the maximum and reports it: a `WP_CLI::warning()` on
the command line and a notice on the demo-data page, each naming the field, the
number asked for and the number used. Refusing outright would be worse — an
operator who asks for too much should still get a run, just an honest one.
