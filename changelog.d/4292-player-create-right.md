# Adding players is for the people who hold the create right; head coaches no longer see the player-limit notice (#4292)

At the free-tier player limit, the players list showed the "you have reached the limit" upgrade notice to coaches, assistants and parents, none of whom can add players. Adding a player, by hand or by CSV import, was checked against the edit right every head coach holds for their own squad. Adding a player now takes the create right on players, held by the Head of Development and the Club Admin. That right decides New player, Import from CSV (on the players and the teams list), the limit notice, the new-player wizard, the create form, the import screen, the wp-admin Add New, `POST /players` and `POST /players/import`. Head coaches still correct their squad's records, and trialists still come in through the prospect pipeline.
