# The read-only observer reads every scouting visit, with the scout's notes masked (#5085)

The read-only observer opens the Scouting visits tile, lists every visit in the club and opens each one read-only: who visited, when, where and which players were seen. The scout's notes read "Hidden" on screen and are left out of the API. A new right, "Scouting visits: read every visit" (`scouting_visits_all`), carries this, and only the observer holds it, so a scout still sees only their own visits and the head of development all of them. Reading every visit adds no write. Migration 0334 adds the observer's two rows on existing installs and leaves a cell an academy changed alone.
