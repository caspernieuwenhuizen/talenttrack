# The read-only observer reads the trials, masked (#4694)

The read-only observer lists and opens every trial case, read-only: the dates, the track, the status, the decision, the panel and the rating of each released input. The case summary, the extension justifications and the input notes read "Hidden". The panel's synthesis, the decision and letter tabs, the letters and every change stay with the trial managers and the panel. Scouts and head coaches list exactly the cases they listed before. Migration 0333 adds the observer's trial read rows on existing installs and leaves a cell an academy changed alone.
