# Report Studio: one document for screen, print and PDF, and snapshots (#5021)

Bump: minor

A Report Studio report in rows is now one document, drawn on the server, that the reader's view, the canvas's A4 view and the new PDF all show, so a block cannot look one way on screen and another on paper. On a phone the same document stacks, with every table on the stack-table primitive. **Make PDF** on the canvas prints the draft, and the reader's view has **Download PDF**. The key figures block is the monthly report's KPI section and now prints with that report's own section renderer and is measured with its heights, so the page meter has one estimate.

A published report about one player or one team can be frozen with **Take snapshot**: the published version for the subject and period on screen, stored in the snapshot table that player or team already has, and readable after the report changes or is archived. A snapshot of a family report is shared with the player and their parents and appears on the player's Reports tab next to the shared player reports, cut to what each of them may see on the child's file. A snapshot for staff opens only for readers who read as staff, and is shown with the reader's own rights, not the rights of whoever took it: a block they could not read live is left out, and so is a row about a player they cannot see. Whoever may take that player's or team's snapshots can write a note under each block, kept with the snapshot, never in the report, and audit-logged. Group and academy reports have no snapshots. The goals and assists block and "your own question" are no longer offered for a report for the family: the player report gives a family neither. Migration 0327 adds `report_id` and `report_version_id` to both snapshot tables; existing rows are unchanged. New REST routes: `GET /reports/{id}/document`, `GET` and `POST /reports/{id}/snapshots`, `GET /report-snapshots/{uuid}` and `PUT /report-snapshots/{uuid}/notes/{block}`, plus the `report_studio_pdf` export.
