# Report Studio: open goals, goals completed and PDP conversations held against planned blocks (#4572)

Bump: minor

The Report Studio gets its goals and PDP blocks. Open goals and goals completed show where a player stands now, not in the report's period, with the same counts as the player's Goals tab and goals overview. For a team, open goals lists every squad player as the team monthly report's squad list does. PDP conversations held against planned is the PDP planning page's row for the team in the current season, block by block: the squad, the talks planned, held and not held, and each block's window. Each block answers to the right of the screen its figures come from: the goals blocks to the goals read, and the PDP block to PDP planning. A family may see a player's own goal counts, a scout never. Tests hold every block to the read it comes from.
