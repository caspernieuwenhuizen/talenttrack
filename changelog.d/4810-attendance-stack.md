# Activity form: attendance and guests read cleanly on a phone and on a desktop (#4810)

On a phone, the planned-attendance and guest tables on the activity form show
each player as a card. A guest's name, badge and home team now stay together
next to the "Player" label, and the Remove link sits under the notes field,
instead of the parts squeezing into narrow columns that broke words mid-way.
On a tablet or desktop the columns are balanced again: the notes field gets
room instead of being squeezed to a sliver, and the guests table uses the
column widths it was designed with. The tables also move onto the shared
stacked-table layout, so a screen reader hears them as tables with their
column headers.
