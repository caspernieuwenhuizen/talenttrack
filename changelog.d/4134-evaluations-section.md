# Monthly report: an Evaluations section (#4134)

Bump: minor

The team monthly report has a new Evaluations section, right after the squad
status: the squad average and how it moved, who was evaluated and who was
not, each main category's average with its change and the spread of the
players on the rating scale, and the three biggest risers and fallers against
their own previous evaluation. Details adds a player-by-category grid in
shirt-number order, each cell coloured along the academy's rating scale with
an arrow against the previous month, optionally with subcategories. The
evaluation types that count can be chosen, and the squad average equals the
"Squad rating" tile for the same types. The pack places the section on page 1
when it fits, otherwise beside the player-by-player table or on a page of its
own; the one-pager and the landscape matrix print the summary.
