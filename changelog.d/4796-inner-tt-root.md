# The guardian contact page and the plan panels follow the academy's theme (#4796)

The page a parent opens from a guardian contact link and the panels that explain a plan limit reset the design colours to their defaults inside themselves. On the Leon Hutten and federation themes, they showed the default greys, text colour and rounded corners instead of the theme's. They now take the theme's values, like every other part of the dashboard. On the default theme the upgrade panels' background and border change very slightly, to the same light grey the rest of the dashboard already uses.
