# The Branding focus colour colours the keyboard focus ring again (#4829)

The "Focus ring color" field under Branding did nothing once the focus ring started following the primary colour. On the default design, a focus colour the academy sets now colours the ring around the active field or button again. Without one, the ring keeps following the primary colour.

A focus colour too faint to see against the page (less than 3 to 1) is still saved, but the form warns that it is hard to see as a focus outline, and the ring keeps the primary-based colour so keyboard focus never disappears. The field now says what it is for.

Under the Leon Hutten and federation themes nothing changes; the theme decides the ring.
