# Monthly report PDF is named after the team and the month (#4141)

**Download PDF** on the team monthly report now saves as the team and the month the report covers, for example `Hedel-O14-1-2026-09.pdf`, instead of `team_monthly_report_pdf-<today>.pdf`. A period over more than one month names both ends (`Hedel-O14-1-2026-08-2026-09.pdf`), and a snapshot's PDF adds the day it was taken (`Hedel-O14-1-2026-09-snapshot-2026-10-02.pdf`). The download and the monthly e-mail's attachment now carry the same name. Other PDF exports keep their current names.
