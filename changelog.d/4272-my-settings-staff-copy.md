# My settings no longer tells staff about "your child" (#4272)

Three hints on My settings were written for parents and showed on every account, so a team manager or an admin read that their messages concern "you or your own child" and that details on "your child's file" are changed by the academy. An account with no linked child now reads that messages concern "you or the players you work with". It also no longer sees the sentences about a child's file. A linked parent sees the same wording as before.
