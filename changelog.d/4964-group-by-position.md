# Report Studio: a group of players by position (#4964)

A report about a group of players can now be narrowed to one position, such as all centre-backs in O14 and O15. A player counts by their first position only, so each player is in one position group and the groups add up to the team. The group step also offers **All my teams**: every team you build reports for, in one tap. The review names the group. Blocks that only work for one player or one team are switched off on a group report with the reason "This block does not work for a group of players", and a group report refuses one when it is saved.
