# Demo PDP dossiers are only closed once their conversations have been held (#3650)

A generated academy stamped roughly three in ten of the current season's PDP
dossiers *completed*, with a signed-off end-of-season verdict, while all four
of their conversations were still in the diary. Reading the demo, a head of
development saw a finished season in the week it started, and PDP coverage
reported everybody covered while almost no conversation had taken place. A
dossier is now closed by its last conversation rather than by chance: early in
a season the demo shows open cycles, which is what that season honestly looks
like. Finished seasons still close every dossier with a verdict, unchanged.
