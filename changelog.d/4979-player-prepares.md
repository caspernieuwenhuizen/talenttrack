# The player prepares a form conversation in My PDP (#4979)

At an academy with PDP conversation forms, the player now prepares the next development talk on the form in My PDP, in place of the free-text self-reflection. It opens 14 days before the planned talk; before that, or for a talk with no planned date, My PDP says when it opens and the form routes answer `409 preparation_not_open`. The form goes one section per step, then the strongest quality: each item shows its definition and five numbered choices with the form's own labels, and everything saves itself. "Klaar" is its own button: it marks the preparation ready and shows a summary of the scores per section and the strongest quality, and "Toch aanpassen" takes it back until the talk starts, after which the summary is read-only. The trainer's form switch no longer shows on a talk that did not take place, and a switch refused there says the form can no longer be switched. The save state on every autosaving screen ("All changes saved", "Saving…", the error) now reads at full contrast.
