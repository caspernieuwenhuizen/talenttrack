# PDP talk: clear a trainer score, and sections that clearly collapse (#5081)

A score on the PDP conversation form can be emptied again: tap the chosen score once more, press the new Clear button beside the row, or press Space on the chosen score. The clear saves (the form routes store it as null), undo brings the score back, and sign-off's "still needed" list names the item again. This lives in the shared numbered-choices rating row, so the player's own preparation on the form clears the same way; a surface can switch it off with `clearable => false`, and disabled rows never offer it. The form's sections now read as folds: each heading is a full-width row with a chevron, an "N items · M of N scored" line that follows the scores, the "to discuss" badge and "Expand" / "Collapse"; "Expand all" and "Collapse all" sit above the sections, and a section opens by itself when one of its fields takes focus.
