# PDP talk: clear a trainer score, and sections that clearly collapse (#5081)

A score on the PDP conversation form can be emptied again: tap the chosen score once more, press the new Clear button beside the row, or press Space on the chosen score, and the form's legend says so ("Tap your score again, or choose Clear, to empty it."). The clear saves (the form routes store it as null, whether `null` or an empty string is sent), undo brings the score back, sign-off's "still needed" list names the item again, and a clear the player makes after "klaar" shows the trainer the item as changed. This lives in the shared numbered-choices rating row, so the player's own preparation clears the same way and carries the same hint; a surface can switch it off with `clearable => false`, and disabled rows never offer it. The form's sections now read as folds: each heading is a full-width row with a chevron, a count line that follows what is filled in ("5 items · 2 of 5 scored"; "Filled in" for the strongest quality; "2 development points · 1 still empty" for the SMART points), the "to discuss" badge and "Expand" / "Collapse"; "Expand all" and "Collapse all" sit above the sections, and a section opens by itself when one of its fields takes focus.
