# Date of birth and guardian contact follow the sensitive-field registry on every player surface (#4691)

The full date of birth is now for staff of the player, the player themselves and a linked parent. Any other reader who may open the player, such as the read-only observer, sees "Hidden" with the birth year beside it on the player profile, the wp-admin player list, record and form, the team roster in wp-admin, the player comparison, the one-pager PDF and the goal intake print, and gets the date left out of the player REST answers and the squad files. `GET /players` and `GET /players/{id}` now carry `birth_year` for every reader. Birthdays stay out of the coach's week for such a reader, since a birthday and the birth year together give the date. The list row's linked parent name follows the staff rule like the other guardian fields. The registry now names where each route's rows sit in its answer, so the REST safety net only reads player rows. Staff, a parent and the player see what they saw before.
