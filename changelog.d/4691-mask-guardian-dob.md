# Date of birth and guardian contact follow the sensitive-field registry on every player surface (#4691)

The full date of birth is now for staff of the player, the player themselves and a linked parent whose access to the child is current. Any other reader who may open the player, such as the read-only observer, sees "Hidden" with the birth year beside it on the player profile, the wp-admin player list, record and form, the team roster in wp-admin, the player comparison, the one-pager PDF and the goal intake print, and gets the date left out of the player REST answers, the potential overview's rows and the squad files. `GET /players`, `GET /players/{id}` and the potential overview carry `birth_year` for every reader. The player peek card shows the full date to a reader who may read it and the birth year to everyone else. Birthdays stay out of the coach's week for such a reader, since a birthday and the birth year together give the date. A guardian's e-mail and phone on `GET /people` and `GET /people/{id}` go to the guardian themselves and to staff of one of their children; the list row's linked parent name and the club's own player fields on the player card follow the staff rule. The registry now names, per route, where the rows sit and which key holds their id, so the REST safety net only reads the rows it is told about. Staff, a parent and the player see what they saw before.
