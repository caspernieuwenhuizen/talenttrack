# A Report Studio schedule to a role nobody holds no longer empties the PDF (#5145)

When a Report Studio schedule went to a role that nobody in the academy holds, the PDF lost every block and was not sent. Now that role leaves the PDF as it is, and **What they get** says no one holds the role. People in a chosen role who have no person record in the academy are now listed with that reason, instead of "not in this academy".
