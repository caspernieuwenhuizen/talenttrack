# The team-limit upgrade notice shows only to people who can add teams (#4286)

On a free-tier install with more teams than the package allows, the teams list showed the "you have reached the limit" upgrade notice to every viewer. Head coaches, who can neither add teams nor change the package, read it as a data error. The notice now shows only to people who could otherwise add a team, the same people who see the New team button below the cap. Below the cap nothing changes.
