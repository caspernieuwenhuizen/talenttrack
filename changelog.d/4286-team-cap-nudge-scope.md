# Adding a team is for the people who hold the create right; head coaches no longer see the team-limit notice (#4286)

On a free-tier install with more teams than the package allows, the teams list showed the "you have reached the limit" upgrade notice to head coaches, who read it as a data error. Creating a team was checked against the edit right that every head coach holds for their own team, so a head coach could also add teams the authorization matrix never granted them. Adding a team now takes the create right on teams, held by the Head of Development and the Club Admin. That right decides the New team action, the limit notice, the new-team wizard, the create form, the wp-admin Add New and `POST /teams`. Head coaches still edit the teams they run.
