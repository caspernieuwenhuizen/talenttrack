# The read-only observer reads status and potential, with the band masked (#4694)

The read-only observer reads each player's status colour and its reasons, and sees that a potential was set, when and by whom. The potential band, how it moved and the notes behind it read "Hidden" on screen and in the player report PDF, and are left out of the API: the potential history, the status breakdown (whose composite score is left empty too, since it would give the band back), the squad potential overview, the profile's Behaviour & potential card, the forecast group of a player's attributes and the KPI export. One rule decides it everywhere. A scout keeps the band of the players they are linked to; the head of development, the admin, head coaches and families read what they read before. Migration 0335 adds the observer's status and potential read rows on existing installs and leaves a cell an academy changed alone.
