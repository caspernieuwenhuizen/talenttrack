# A signed PDP conversation form prints as the academy's A4 form (#4982)

A signed PDP conversation form now has its own printout, as a print page and as a PDF from the same renderer, and it fits on one A4. It shows the header (player, date, team, trainer), the scale, every item with the player's (S) and the trainer's (T) score, the trainer's notes, the strongest quality, the two development points with their deadlines, and who confirmed it when, in place of signature lines. An empty player column reads "not filled in", and a confirmation not yet given reads "not yet confirmed". Nothing of an unfinished form prints. **Print** sits under the signed form: on a phone it opens the PDF. Who may print it is who may read the form on screen. A talk without a form still prints its whole file, unchanged.
