# Activity, VCT, methodology and learning tables on a phone read like every other table (#4916)

The opponent backfill, the VCT reference templates and exercise library, the methodology periodisation and formation positions, and the learning reports per course, per person, per team and for your own record now use the shared table layout. On a phone each row is a card with each value beside its column's label, as on the other screens that have moved; in the learning reports the course, person or team heads its card. The opponent backfill no longer scrolls sideways on a phone, its fields fill the card, and the exercise library's Edit and Archive buttons wrap inside the card instead of running off it. From 480px wide nothing changes. Screen readers read each table as a table with its column headers.
