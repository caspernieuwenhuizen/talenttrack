# My journey now speaks to the player reading it (#3398)

The journey screen backs two addresses — a coach opening a player's timeline,
and the player opening their own — and rendered the same text to both. A player
saw their own name in the third person under a heading that said "Mijn reis", a
description of the page as a story "for this player", and a pointer to "the
parent meeting", which is a staff appointment they do not attend.

The player now gets their own heading and description. When entries on their
timeline are visible only to coaching staff, the count still appears — a
timeline with silent gaps would be its own kind of dishonesty — but it now says
plainly who can see them and that their coach is the person to ask. The three
always-visible filters are theirs too: what a coach wrote, what they were set to
work on, and moving up an age group, in place of a trials filter that is empty
for a signed player.

The welcome card a parent sees also said "you choose what your child shares with
you". The player chooses; it now says so.
