# Role names in sentence case, from one list (#4523)

Role names now follow the sentence case the rest of the interface uses: "Club admin", "Head coach", "Assistant coach", "Team member", "Head of development" and "Read-only observer". That covers the roles admin page, the functional roles page, the People admin page, the roles explainer and the staff picker. The "Functional roles" and "Permission debug" page titles and links follow suit. Those screens used to keep their own copies of the role names, which is how they drifted. They now all read one list. The Dutch is unchanged, except that the observer role now reads "Alleen-lezen waarnemer" with a lower-case w. On the People admin page, the `manager` role type now reads "Team manager", as it already did on the frontend People list.
