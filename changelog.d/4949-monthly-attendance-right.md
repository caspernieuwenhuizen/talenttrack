# The monthly team report's attendance follows the attendance right (#4949)

The attendance section of the team monthly report and its attendance headline number are now shown only to someone who may read that team's attendance, the same right the attendance reports ask. Someone who may read the team's reports but not its attendance, such as the read-only observer, gets the rest of the report without them, on screen, in print and in the PDF. The Report Studio's key figures follow the same rule.
