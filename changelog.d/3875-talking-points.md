# Player report: talking points (#3875)

The player report's **Talking points** section now says what a conversation
with the player should raise, worked out from what the academy has recorded:
the status model's flag and reasons, a clear drop in attendance against the
period before, playing time below the academy's minutes target, a long period
without an evaluation, a test that moved the wrong way, a goal past its due
date, a return from injury, a move to another age group, team or position, and
gaps in the record the status model wanted. The most urgent come first, the
wording is evidential rather than a judgement, and nothing is raised on too
little data — a handful of sessions or a couple of matches is not a trend.
