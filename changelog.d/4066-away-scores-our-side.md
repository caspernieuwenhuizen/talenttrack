# Away results no longer read reversed in the monthly report, team statistics and minutes grid (#4066)

An away match we won 3-1 showed as a 1-3 defeat in the team monthly report (page and PDF) and in the team statistics tab, and counted as a loss in the record and in goals for / against. Since #3530 the stored home score is always the academy's goals whatever the venue, but three readers still swapped the pair on an away match. All three now read the score through one shared helper, so home / away is only a label. The minutes grid had the same swap on the way in: on an away match its "Our goals" box wrote into the opponent's column. Away scores typed in the minutes grid before this fix are stored reversed and now show reversed in the grid, so check your away matches there and correct any that read the wrong way round.
