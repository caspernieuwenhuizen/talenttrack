# Evaluation comments follow the sensitive-field registry (#4693)

An evaluation's written words now go only to the readers they are for. The staff-only notes go to staff for the player and to whoever may write that player's evaluations; the player feedback and the category notes go to those readers and to the player and a linked parent, as before. Every other reader of the evaluation, such as the read-only observer, keeps the ratings, dates, type and evaluator, and sees "Hidden" where a note would be on the evaluation screen, in wp-admin and in the player report; the evaluation REST routes leave the fields out and the evaluation files leave the cells empty. The read-only observer reads the internal player report, without the notes. The staff-only notes on `GET /evaluations/{id}` now go to staff only, matching their description. Staff and family see what they saw before.
