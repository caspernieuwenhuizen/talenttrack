# Reports: trial decisions show again (#4856)

The trial decisions figures in analytics and Report Studio, and the trial counts on the season summary, the trial funnel and the scout report card, read a decision date the trial record doesn't have, so they came out empty or zero. They now read when the decision was made and who made it, so the period filter, the per-month view and the "Decided by" breakdown show the academy's real decisions.
