# Player journey: a PDP verdict entry follows the PDP verdict right (#4775)

A PDP verdict entry on a player's journey shows only to a reader who may read that player's PDP verdict, and to a parent only while the child shares the PDP with them. The rule holds on the timeline and transitions routes, the profile's Journey tab, cohort transitions, the My development journey card, the player report's journey block and the team monthly report's "what changed" list. Every other entry is unchanged, and an unattended report render gets no verdict entry.
