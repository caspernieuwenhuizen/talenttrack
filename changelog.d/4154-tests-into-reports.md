# Test trends and BMI-for-age move from dashboard tiles into Reports (#4154)

Test trends and Player · BMI-for-age no longer have their own tiles in the dashboard's Analyses group. They are listed on the Reports page, in a new Tests & measurements section after Development & performance, which is where both reports already pointed their breadcrumb. The entries keep the tiles' access check: they appear only to someone who may read test results, only while the Measurements module is on, and each disappears when its report is switched off under Features. Test results stays a dashboard tile.
