# PDP talk: the player in the header, Background, and the form in the tab bar (#5079)

The PDP conversation page now names whose talk it is and which one. The record spine carries the player's photo or initials, name, team, shirt number and position, with one line for the talk ("Mid season · conversation 2 of 3 · planned …", or "held …" once it has taken place), and the breadcrumb ends in the player's name, then the talk. The Evidence tab is called Background, and the verdict's disclosure reads "Background for this season". The form a talk uses, and the switch to the other form while switching is still possible, sit on the right of the tab row instead of in a block above the talk; on a phone they wrap under the tabs. `RecordSpine` gains two optional keys for this, `detail` and `aside`; a strip without them renders as before.
