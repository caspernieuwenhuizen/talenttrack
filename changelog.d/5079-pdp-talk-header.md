# PDP talk: the player in the header, Background, and the form in the tab bar (#5079)

The PDP conversation page now opens on whose talk it is and which one: a hero, in both shells, with the player's photo or initials, name, team, shirt number and position, and one line for the talk ("Mid season · conversation 2 of 3 · planned …", or "held …" once it has taken place). Under the app shell the record spine carries the same identity as the strip that stays in view while scrolling, and the breadcrumb ends in the player's name, then the talk. The Evidence tab is called Background, and the verdict's disclosure reads "Background for this season". The form a talk uses, and the switch to the other form while switching is still possible, sit unframed on the right of the tab row instead of in a block above the talk; on a phone they wrap under the tabs. `RecordSpine` gains two optional keys for this, `detail` and `aside`; a strip without them renders as before. Under the app shell a stuck spine now sits below the shell's sticky header instead of sliding under it.
