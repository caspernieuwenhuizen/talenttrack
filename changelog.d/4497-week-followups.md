# This week panel: follow-ups due - PDP talks, evaluations, my tasks (#4497)

The **This week** panel can show a **Follow-ups due this week** section below its list, one line per follow-up, each linking to where it is dealt with. **PDP conversations due** counts the PDP talks still to hold in a block that closes this week, with the closing day, the same count PDP planning shows. **Evaluations due** counts the players not yet evaluated in the evaluation round that is open now, as Evaluation coverage marks them. **My tasks due this week** counts your own open tasks due in the window. A line shows only when something is due and you may open the page it links to, and the section hides when every line is empty. The three blocks sit in a new **Follow-ups due this week** section of the panel settings, on by default for the head coach, the head of development, the team manager and the academy admin as listed in the docs. `GET /me/week` returns them as `pdp_due`, `evals_due` and `tasks_due`.
