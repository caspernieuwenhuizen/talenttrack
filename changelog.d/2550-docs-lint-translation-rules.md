Completes the docs-lint gate with the two translation rules held back until the
Dutch corpus reached parity: every reader- or admin-facing topic must have a
Dutch twin, and that twin's title and summary must actually be translated while
its group, audience and order match the English.

Between them these stop the gap the help corpus had before — a Dutch academy
silently reading English because a topic was never translated and nothing
noticed.
