# One chokepoint for sensitive fields: RecordRedactor and the field registry (#4690)

Sensitive fields on a player-linked record are now listed in one registry, `config/sensitive_fields.php`, each with the right that unlocks it, and hidden in one place, `RecordRedactor`. A REST answer from a registered route is redacted after the controller has built it, so a reader without the right gets the answer without the field, whichever controller produced it. Views get a shared "Hidden" marker (`MaskedValue`) with a screen-reader label, "Hidden for your role". The player record's guardian contact and custom fields are the first fields registered and follow the same staff rule as before; nothing a reader sees changes in this release. Absence reasons, evaluation comments, date of birth and the trial and scouting fields move onto the redactor with their own surfaces.
