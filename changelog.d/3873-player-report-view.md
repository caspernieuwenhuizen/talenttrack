# Player report on screen (#3873)

Bump: minor

A new report, **Player · Report**, puts one player's season so far on one page
for a conversation with them: status, talking points, evaluations, attendance,
playing time, goals, the development plan and space to write notes, with match
by match, tests, journey, injuries, behaviour, potential and staff notes one
tick away. Open it from the **Player card** tab on a player's file, or from
Reports → Development & performance. It opens on the season so far, works for a
player who has no development plan yet, and a saved view keeps your sections
without tying them to one player. Staff only — each section shows only what
your role may see on the player's file.
