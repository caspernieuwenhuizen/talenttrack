# The monthly report can carry per-section settings (#3514)

Groundwork, invisible on screen until the next release in this series.

A monthly report was composed by ticking sections on or off, and everything
else about a section was decided for you. Asking for *one* of the tests you
ran, or for the readings rather than the summary, could not be expressed —
not in the link you copy, not in a saved view, not in a monthly schedule.

A composition now carries settings per section alongside the section list, and
they travel everywhere a composition already travelled: the address in your
browser, your saved views, the PDF link, and the monthly schedule's own copy.

Nothing changes in any existing report. A composition with no settings — which
is every one saved so far — renders exactly as it did, and a view saved before
this release still opens.
