# Report Studio: results and team statistics blocks; "Played" means one thing everywhere (#4572)

Bump: minor

The Report Studio gets its match blocks, which complete the first wave. Results lists every match in the period with its score, as the team monthly report's match section does, with the record under it. Team statistics is the team Statistics tab's record. "Played" now has one meaning on every screen: the matches that are completed, in the past, or have minutes recorded, the rule the minutes reports already use. The monthly report used to print every fixture in its window under that label, the one next week included, and the Statistics tab only the matches with a score. Both now print the same figure, and a played match nobody typed a score into counts as played. The statistics block answers to the right to read the team, as the Statistics tab does. A test holds the monthly report's record and the tab's record to the same figures.
