# Match prep chip colours, phone roster card and small fixes (#4131)

Under the 2026 match-prep styling, a selected Excused, Absent, Injured or
Suspended chip keeps its own colour again instead of all showing the club
colour, so the coach can tell them apart at a glance. On a phone, the monthly
report's player-by-player card sets its seven figures four and three, so the
Suspended figure no longer sits on a row of its own. The team match statistics
query drops a cancelled check that its shared clause already covers, and the
media section of the authorization matrix docs labels its column "Access".
