# Player report: journey entries say which activity they were about (#3963)

In the player report's **Journey** section, a comment from a match analysis
and an evaluation made for a training or match now name that activity: its
type, the opponent (or its title) and the date, for example "Wedstrijd · tegen
Willem II · 12 september 2026". On screen it links to the activity for staff who
can open activities; the PDF prints it after the entry, and the page count
includes it.
