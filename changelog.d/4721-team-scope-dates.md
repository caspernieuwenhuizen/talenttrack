# A coach's teams are the teams they work with today (#4721)

A staff assignment's start and end dates now decide whether the team counts. If the assignment has ended, or has not started yet, the team drops out of the person's team lists, their players and what they may open. It drops out or comes in on that day, with nothing to run.

The update dates the existing assignments in the same way. A person with several assignments on one team keeps that team from the earliest start to the latest end. Team access an administrator set by hand keeps its own dates.
