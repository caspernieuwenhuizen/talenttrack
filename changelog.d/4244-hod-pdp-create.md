# Head of Development can open a new PDP file from the planning screen (#4244)

The "Open new PDP file" form now lists every player for anyone who may change any PDP file, such as the Head of Development, instead of only for settings admins. Before, a Head of Development who coaches no team followed a "Missing" link on PDP planning and saw "No players available", although the API would have created the file. Coaches still see only the players on their own teams.
