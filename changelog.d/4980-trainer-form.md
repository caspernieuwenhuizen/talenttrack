# The trainer fills in and signs a PDP conversation form (#4980)

On the conversation screen, a talk that uses a conversation form now shows the form on the Conversation tab. Each item has the trainer's five numbered choices with the player's score marked on the same row (light blue). Before the player marks the preparation ready, every item says "Speler: nog niet klaar" and none of the player's answers shows. "Bespreken" marks every item where the two scores differ, "aangepast" marks a score the player changed after marking ready, and the sections are folds with a notes field each. The strongest quality shows what the player wrote above what was agreed, and the two SMART development points take an action, the trainer's help, a deadline and an optional item. Everything saves itself. "Gesprek afronden en ondertekenen" is its own button with a confirm: it stays closed until the form is complete and lists what is missing, does not wait for the player's preparation, and on sign-off saves the evaluation and creates the two goals. A score or answer sent again unchanged no longer counts as a change.
