# A scheduled report contains only what every recipient may read (#4991)

A scheduled report is one PDF for all its recipients, and every section of it now contains only what every recipient may read, the person who set up the schedule included. In the monthly team report, when one recipient may not see the team's attendance, the attendance section, the attendance headline number and the attendance figures in the other sections are left out for everyone; "What changed" lists only the journey entries every recipient may see, so an entry kept to coaching staff, medical staff or safeguarding, or a PDP verdict, appears only when all of them may see it, and the injury column and the number of players currently injured appear only when all of them may read the injury records; and "Tests" shows a test only when all of them may see tests at its level. The scheduled player report follows the same rule for the journey, the development plan, the staff notes, the injuries, the tests and the written notes on evaluations. A recipient whose rights cannot be checked (an address with no account, a role member from outside the academy, a role with no members) counts as having none. The recipients are checked again at every run, so a lost right shrinks the next PDF. The schedules screen shows under each monthly report what its recipients get, and both schedule forms state the rule.
