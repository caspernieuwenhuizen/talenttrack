# The VCT training designer offers the Head of Development a team to pick (#4024)

Step 1 of the designer built its team list from a WordPress settings
capability: hold it and you saw every team, otherwise you saw the teams you
were assigned to as a coach. The Head of Development holds neither — the
role deliberately gave up settings access, and a head of development coaches
no squad — so the select contained nothing but "— pick a team —", Next stayed
disabled, and the designer was unusable for the one person whose job is
planning conditioning across the academy.

The picker now lists the teams you may actually create a session for, asked
per team: your own squads as a coach, every academy team as Head of
Development or academy admin. That is the same question the wizard asks when
you submit, so the list can no longer offer a team the submit refuses — the
other half of the same bug.

When there genuinely is no such team, the step says so and names the access
that is missing, instead of leaving you on a required field with nothing in
it.
