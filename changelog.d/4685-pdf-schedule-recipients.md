# Scheduled PDF reports: the form says who receives the report (#4685)

The schedule forms for the team monthly report and the player report said only "Send it to staff only.", although every recipient is checked when the report is sent. Each form now explains it: an account receives the PDF only when it may read that team's reports, or the report about every player in it; an address with no account here receives nothing; and whoever is left out is named on the schedule after the run. Wording only; who receives the report is unchanged.
