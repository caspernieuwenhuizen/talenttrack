# Match execution spots a half that was left running (#3667)

The live match screen now shows who started the match and when the half began, right under the clock. When a half has run longer than its length plus 10 minutes, for example because someone tapped Start and put the phone away, the screen warns that it looks left running. It offers **End half at scheduled length**, which ends the half at exactly its planned length, and **Record the match afterwards**. Every end of a half is also capped at its length plus 10 minutes, so a forgotten clock no longer freezes the half time at hundreds of minutes or blocks the events logged afterwards. Over REST, `GET /match-execution/{activity_id}/clock` returns the clock with `overrun`, `limit_seconds`, `started_at` and `started_by`, and `end-half` takes `at: scheduled`.
