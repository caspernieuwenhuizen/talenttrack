# Player notes follow the scout's linked-player scope and the staff-only rule (#5059)

A scout reads the notes kept about a player only for the players they may view, on the player page, in search and over REST alike: the notes thread asks that the reader may open the player's record. A scout never sees a note marked staff only, with the authorization matrix on or off: the evaluation-change capability counts towards staff-only notes only on a team the reader coaches. The Notes tab badge on the player page counts a staff-only note only for a reader who may see it. Migration 0328 narrows the scout's default conversation row on existing installs, leaves a row an academy changed itself alone, and says so in its note. Coaches, the head of development and parents keep the access they had.
