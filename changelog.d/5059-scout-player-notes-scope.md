# Player notes follow the scout's linked players and the staff-only rule (#5059)

A scout reads the notes kept about a player only for the players they are linked to through a trial panel seat or the scout assignment list, on the player page, in search and over REST alike: the notes thread asks that the reader may open the player's record. The scout's conversation read is held at player scope, so a scout never sees a note marked staff only. Migration 0328 narrows the scout's default row on existing installs and leaves a row an academy changed itself alone. The Notes tab badge on the player page counts a staff-only note only for a reader who may see it. Coaches, the head of development and parents keep the access they had.
