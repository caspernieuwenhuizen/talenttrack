# Monthly report: meeting snapshots, with notes per section (#3517)

Bump: minor

The monthly report shows current data, which a staff meeting cannot run on: open
it on the 3rd, discuss it on the 5th, and a register taken in between has moved
the numbers under the discussion.

**Save snapshot for the meeting** freezes what you are looking at. A snapshot
keeps the numbers as they were and has its own address, so reopening it next
season shows what the meeting actually saw. Each section can carry one typed
note, and those notes stay editable after the snapshot is taken while the numbers
do not — you draft before the meeting and write down what was decided during it.
Every note records who last wrote it and when, and the PDF prints them, so the
printed copy and the screen say the same thing.

**A snapshot cannot be shared by link.** It is readable only by someone signed in
who can already see that team's reports — no share token, no public address. This
is deliberately different from match prep and match analysis: a monthly report
names every player in the squad alongside their attendance, their test results
and who needs a conversation, and a link that works for anyone holding it is the
wrong shape for that. Staff who do not log in get the PDF, handed over by someone
who does.
