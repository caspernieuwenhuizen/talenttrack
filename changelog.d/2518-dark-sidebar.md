# The navigation sidebar is now dark, matching the design (#2518)

Bump: patch

With the app shell switched on, the navigation sidebar now renders in dark navy
with light text, as the approved design specifies, instead of white. The group
headings, hover and selected states and the collapsed icon rail all follow, and
the drawer that the sidebar becomes on a phone takes the same treatment.

The selected item keeps its accent stripe, which now uses your academy's own
brand colour where one is configured rather than a fixed shade.

Nothing moved: the academy name stays in the top bar and your account menu
stays in its corner, so logging out is where it has always been. Only the
colours changed.

Classic navigation is untouched.
