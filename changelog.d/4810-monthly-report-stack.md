# Team monthly report: the stacked tables read as tables (#4810)

On a phone or tablet, the team monthly report's player-by-player roster, the
evaluation grid and the ranked test readings stay the cards they were, each
figure with its name. They now use the shared stacked-table layout, so a
screen reader hears each one as a table with its column headers. From 1024
pixels wide the report is the printed sheet, as before, and the PDF is
unchanged.
