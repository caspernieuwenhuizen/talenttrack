# Report Studio, phase 2: the builder canvas in Rows (#4571)

Bump: minor

The Report Studio tile under Reports lists the reports a builder made, with the **Add report** button that opens the start wizard (started from a player's or team's page, it begins about them; it is hidden when the wizard is switched off). The wizard now lands on the canvas at `?tt_view=report-studio&id=N`. Blocks show live figures for the report's subject and period, read in one request, and an empty block says why. A builder adds blocks by tap or by dragging, reorders them by drag or with the arrows, makes them full or half width, duplicates and removes them, and changes their settings, which say in plain words where the figures come from and whether a family may see the block. Blocks that do not fit stay in the library, switched off, with the reason. The context bar switches to another of the builder's own players or teams, or another period, without saving it; **Read as** previews the report as the family or a scout would get it, cut on the server; the A4 view shows the page breaks and how full the pages are, from the player report's page estimate. The draft saves itself through the shared autosave, with Undo up to 50 steps in the sitting (the one exception to one-step Undo) and Revert. A save from an older tab is refused and that tab reloads the newer draft. **Publish** is its own control: it asks what changed and says how many people it reaches. **Versions** puts an earlier version back in the draft. New routes `GET /reports/{id}/reach` and `GET /reports/subject-options`; the data route takes `audience` and returns `page_fit`; `GET /report-blocks` lists the blocks that do not fit with their reason; a block carries `width`, and a report holds at most 40 blocks. The Studio stays switched off by default and ships in the same release as the start wizard.
