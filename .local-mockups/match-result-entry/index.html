Recording a match result without the live match sheet

Today tt_activities.home_score / away_score has exactly one writer in the whole plugin: MatchExecutionRestController::route_finish(). A club that does its admin on a Sunday evening has no way at all to record that the match ended 3–1 — and no way to record the opponent's goals, which are not player-attributable and so were never reachable through the minutes grid's G column. The same audit found opponent and home_away are read in eight places and written nowhere: the activity create/update payload does not carry them.

The proposal: the result belongs to the match record. A Result card on the match's own page is the primary surface (A); the minutes grid gains two score rows for bulk catch-up (B). A stripped-down second match-execution surface (C) is rejected.

Doelpuntenmakers

Why it does not earn its place

  • It is not an execution. No clock, no halves, no substitution log, no state machine — the entire value of tt_match_execution is that it is running while the match runs. Strip that out and what is left is two number boxes.
  • It needs a mode decision before kick-off. "Live sheet or lite sheet?" is a question the coach has to answer in advance, and get wrong at least once. The Result card needs no decision: it is editable if nobody ran the sheet, and a readout if somebody did.
  • Two writers, one field. A second surface writing home_score is exactly the divergence #2857 removed, re-introduced — the scoreboard reading 3–1 over a goal list holding one goal.
  • The scorer list is already built. The G / A columns of the minutes grid (#3094) do this, for a whole period at once, with reversal semantics and a reconciliation footer. Rebuilding it per match is duplicate surface for less function.
  • It costs a route, a slug, a help topic, a FeatureRegistry entry and a capability — for a payload of two integers that the match record already has columns for.

What a genuinely thin surface looks like instead

The Result card in A, in its Played, no result yet state: one button, two boxes, Save. Same two integers, on the record they belong to, with no new route and no mode to pick.
