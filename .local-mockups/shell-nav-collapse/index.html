Collapsible nav groups — four options

Each sidebar below is live: click a group heading to fold it. All four use the same slide-down transition and the same default — only the group holding the current page starts open (here, Personen, matching Mijn teams). The colours trace the target design; the only thing that differs between the four is how a group says “I can fold”.

Option A

Chevron on hover only

At rest: identical to the design. The chevron fades in when you hover or keyboard-focus a heading.

Truest to the mockup — nothing added at rest.

Nothing hints a group folds until you hover. Invisible on touch, where there is no hover at all.

Option B

Leading caret

A small triangle left of the label, always visible, rotating when open. Reads as a foldable tree.

Instantly legible, works on touch, sits in the indent the labels already leave.

Adds a mark to every heading, and shifts the labels right of the design's alignment.

Option C

Count when collapsed

No chevron anywhere. A folded group shows how many destinations are tucked behind it; open groups show nothing.

Open groups are pixel-identical to the design, and the number tells you what you are missing, not just that something folds.

A numeral is a weaker “click me” than an arrow.

Option D

Full row, divider, chevron

The heading becomes a proper button row: hairline divider above, chevron pinned right, hover fill across the width.

Most obviously clickable and the biggest hit area.

Heaviest of the four — the dividers and permanent chevrons pull the sidebar away from the design's light touch.

Shared across all four: <details>/<summary> so folding works with JS off and announces its own state; a 40px+ hit area on the heading; prefers-reduced-motion honoured; and in the collapsed icon rail every group is forced open, since a folded group there would hide its icons with no heading left to click.
